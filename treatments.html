Treatments · Skin Solutions MD

Seven areas of care.

Treatment follows assessment. If something is not appropriate for your skin, we will say so.

01 General dermatology 02 Acne 03 Pigmentation 04 Laser 05 Anti-ageing 06 Cosmetic 07 Consultations
01

General dermatology

Diagnosis and medical treatment for conditions of the skin, hair and nails. This is the foundation of the clinic and the starting point for most patients.

What it covers
  • Eczema and dermatitis
  • Psoriasis
  • Fungal and bacterial infections
  • Hives and allergic rashes
  • Vitiligo
  • Hair fall and scalp conditions
  • Nail conditions
  • Mole and lesion checks
02

Acne and scarring

Acne is treated according to type and cause, not with a single product for everyone. The plan changes if the acne is hormonal or if scarring has already formed.

What it covers
  • Comedonal acne
  • Inflammatory acne
  • Hormonal acne
  • Post-acne marks
  • Acne scarring
  • Acne in adults
03

Pigmentation

Dark patches and uneven tone have different causes and need different treatment. Melasma, for example, is managed rather than cured, and needs sun protection to hold.

What it covers
  • Melasma
  • Sun-induced pigmentation
  • Post-inflammatory marks
  • Freckles
  • Uneven skin tone
04

Laser hair removal

Hair reduction for the face and body. Settings are planned around skin type and hair colour, and a test area is usually treated before a full session.

Laser works on dark hair. Grey, white and very light hair respond poorly, and no laser will remove hair permanently.

Areas treated
  • Face and neck
  • Underarms
  • Arms
  • Legs
  • Back and chest
  • Ingrown hair
04 / In a session

Settings are chosen for the skin, not the area.

A patch is tested before a full session, and the settings are adjusted to your skin tone and hair colour.

  • Hair Dark hair responds best
  • Skin Tone checked before settings
  • Test patch Before the first full session
  • Eyes Protected for patient and clinician
05

Anti-ageing

Treatment for the changes that come with age and sun exposure: fine lines, loss of firmness, dullness and texture. Medical and non-surgical options are discussed together.

What it covers
  • Fine lines
  • Skin laxity
  • Dullness and texture
  • Under-eye appearance
  • Neck and hands
06

Cosmetic dermatology

Procedures for skin quality: peels, microneedling and rejuvenation. Each one is planned for a specific concern, with the number of sessions set in advance.

What it covers
  • Chemical peels
  • Microneedling
  • Skin rejuvenation
  • Open pores and oil control
07

Consultations

An assessment, a diagnosis and a written plan. Come for a first opinion, a second opinion or a review of treatment you are already having.

What it covers
  • First consultation
  • Second opinion
  • Follow-up review
  • Assessment before a procedure

Not sure which treatment you need?

Start with a consultation. The treatment is decided after the skin has been examined, not before.
