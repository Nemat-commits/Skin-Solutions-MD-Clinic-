Treatments

What we treat,
and how.

Seven areas of care, in plain language. If you are unsure where your concern fits, that is what the consultation is for — send us a message and we'll point you in the right direction.

Every treatment starts with an assessment. We do not book procedures without seeing the skin first.

Ask about a treatment

01

General dermatology

Rashes, infections, eczema, psoriasis, vitiligo, nail problems and moles that need a closer look — the everyday medicine side of dermatology.

What we treat

  • Eczema and contact dermatitis
  • Psoriasis and other scaling conditions
  • Fungal and bacterial skin infections
  • Hives, allergies and reactions
  • Vitiligo and pigment disorders
  • Hair fall, scalp and nail complaints
  • Mole checks and skin cancer screening

What to expect

  • A full history, including medication and products
  • Examination with a dermatoscope where needed
  • A working diagnosis explained in plain language
  • A biopsy or referral only when it changes the plan
  • A review window rather than an open-ended prescription

Bring a list of everything you currently apply to your skin — including things bought without a prescription.

02

Acne & scarring

Acne is treatable at every stage. What changes is the plan: teenage acne, adult hormonal acne and deep scarring each need a different approach.

What we treat

  • Comedonal and inflammatory acne
  • Hormonal and adult acne
  • Acne during pregnancy, with safe options
  • Post-inflammatory marks and redness
  • Ice-pick, boxcar and rolling scars

What to expect

  • An assessment of severity and scarring type
  • A routine short enough to actually keep up with
  • Medical treatment first; procedures only where they earn their place
  • A review at six to eight weeks to adjust the plan
  • Honest timelines — acne settles over months, not days

Most routines fail for a simple reason: too many products, applied inconsistently. We start with fewer steps, chosen properly.

03

Pigmentation & uneven tone

Melasma, sun damage and dark patches after acne are common in Pakistani skin. They respond best to patience, sun protection and the right actives — roughly in that order.

What we treat

  • Melasma and pregnancy-related pigmentation
  • Sun-induced pigmentation and freckles
  • Dark marks left behind by acne
  • Uneven tone and dullness
  • Darkening around the eyes and mouth

What to expect

  • An assessment of your skin type and triggers
  • A sunscreen-first routine, because nothing works without it
  • Prescription creams, and peels where appropriate
  • A realistic timeline: usually months, not weeks
  • Maintenance, because melasma can return

We do not offer whitening injections or bleaching treatments. Brightening here means treating pigment — not changing who you are.

04

Laser hair removal

Hair reduction for the face and body, with settings chosen for your skin tone rather than a one-size protocol.

What we treat

  • Upper lip, chin and face
  • Underarms and arms
  • Legs and bikini line
  • Back, chest and shoulders
  • Ingrown hair and mild folliculitis

What to expect

  • A patch test before your first full session
  • Shaved skin on the day — no waxing or plucking beforehand
  • Sessions spaced four to six weeks apart
  • Six to eight sessions on average; hormonal areas often need more
  • Sun protection and a simple aftercare routine

Laser works best on dark hair. Grey, white and very fine hair usually respond poorly, and we will tell you before you pay for a course.

05

Anti-ageing & rejuvenation

Fine lines, laxity and dullness treated conservatively — enough to look rested, not enough for anyone to guess.

What we treat

  • Fine lines and expression lines
  • Early skin laxity
  • Dullness, rough texture and enlarged pores
  • Under-eye shadows and hollowing
  • Ageing skin on the neck and hands

What to expect

  • An honest conversation about what each option can and cannot do
  • A staged plan rather than six treatments in one sitting
  • Skin preparation before any resurfacing
  • Downtime explained before you agree, not after
  • A maintenance rhythm you can afford to keep

We start with the smallest effective treatment, and only build if you actually need more.

06

Cosmetic dermatology

Medical-grade peels, microneedling, skin brightening and rejuvenation — done in a clinic, with a diagnosis behind them.

What we treat

  • Chemical peels for tone and texture
  • Microneedling for scars and fine lines
  • Skin brightening and dullness
  • Open pores and oil control
  • Pre-event skin preparation

What to expect

  • A patch test before your first peel
  • A course, rather than a single dramatic session
  • Downtime discussed in advance, including what to avoid
  • No treatments on irritated, infected or recently tanned skin
  • Fees confirmed before the course begins

Cosmetic care is still medical care. If your skin isn't ready for a treatment, we'll postpone it rather than push through.

07

Consultations & follow-up

One-to-one time with a dermatologist — for a first opinion, a second opinion, a flare-up, or skin you have been quietly worried about.

What we cover

  • First consultations
  • Second opinions on an existing plan
  • Follow-up reviews and dose adjustments
  • Pre-treatment assessments
  • Advice for family members

What to expect

  • Around 20–30 minutes of unhurried consultation
  • History, examination and a working diagnosis
  • A written plan and a cost estimate before you commit
  • A clear follow-up window
  • Photos, with your consent, to track progress

If you have seen another doctor, bring your prescriptions and any reports. It saves time, repeat tests and money.
