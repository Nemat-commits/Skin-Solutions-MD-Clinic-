02 / Treatments

What we treat

Seven areas, from medical dermatology to laser and cosmetic care. Every one of them begins with a consultation.

01 General dermatology Eczema, psoriasis, infections, rashes, moles and conditions of the hair and nails. 02 Acne and scarring Active acne, hormonal acne, post-inflammatory marks and scarring. 03 Pigmentation Melasma, sun damage, dark spots and uneven skin tone. 04 Laser hair removal Hair reduction for the face and body, planned around skin type and hair colour. 05 Anti-ageing Fine lines, skin laxity, dullness and texture. 06 Cosmetic dermatology Chemical peels, microneedling and skin rejuvenation. 07 Consultations Assessment, diagnosis and a plan for treatment.
03 / At a consultation

How a visit runs

A history, an examination and a plan. Nothing is recommended before the skin has been assessed.

01History

What you tell us

Current products, medication, previous treatments and how long the problem has been present.

02Examination

What we look at

The skin is examined directly. A dermatoscope is used where a lesion or patch needs a closer look.

03Plan

What happens next

You are told what we think is happening, what the options are and what to do next.
