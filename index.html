03 / Approach

At a consultation

A history, an examination and a plan. Nothing is recommended before the skin has been assessed.

The first appointment is for assessment. We look at what is happening in the skin and explain the options before anything is recommended.

  1. 01

    History

    Current products, medication, previous treatments and how long the problem has been present.

  2. 02

    Examination

    The skin is examined directly. A dermatoscope is used where a lesion or patch needs a closer look.

  3. 03

    Plan

    You are told what we think is happening, what the options are and what to do next.
