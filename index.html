02 Our approach

A diagnosis before a prescription.

Skin is rarely a quick fix. The conditions we treat most — acne, melasma, hair loss — are influenced by hormones, sun, products and stress. We look at all of it before we write anything down.

  • 01
    Listen first. Your history explains more than a close-up photograph. We ask about products, medication, hormones and what has already failed.
  • 02
    Treat the cause. A cream can quiet a rash and still leave the reason untouched. We look for the reason.
  • 03
    Say what won't work. If a treatment is unlikely to help, we'll tell you — including when the honest answer is to wait and watch.

Laser hair removal

Hair reduction that respects your skin type.

Laser is not one setting for every person. We assess your skin tone, hair colour and history first, then choose the settings and the spacing that suit you.

  • A patch test before your first full session, every time.
  • Workable for deeper skin tones when the device and settings are chosen properly.
  • Most people need a course of sessions, spaced four to six weeks apart.

03 Your first visit

What actually happens, step by step.

No forms to fill in twice, no ten-minute rush. Here is the shape of a first appointment.

01

Before you arrive

A short WhatsApp message is enough to book. Tell us what is bothering you, and send a photo if it is on your face, so we can set aside the right amount of time.

02

The consultation

The dermatologist takes a full history and examines your skin — with a dermatoscope where a mole or patch needs a closer look — then explains what they think is going on, in plain language.

03

The plan

You leave with a written plan: what to use, what to stop, what a course of treatment costs, and when to come back. There is no obligation to book anything on the spot.
