Approach · Skin Solutions MD

How a consultation works.

The clinic sees patients by appointment, in Blue Area. An appointment is for assessment first.

Most skin problems are diagnosed by looking and listening. The appointment begins with a history, moves to an examination, and ends with a plan you can follow.

  1. 01

    History

    How long the problem has been present, what has already been tried, medication, and anything that makes it better or worse.

  2. 02

    Examination

    The skin is examined directly. A dermatoscope is used for lesions, pigmentation and hair or scalp problems that need a closer look.

  3. 03

    Plan

    You are told what we think is happening, what the options are, and what is expected of each. Written instructions are given where they help.

  4. 04

    Review

    Plans are reviewed. If a treatment is not working, it is changed rather than continued.

04 / Before you come

What to bring

A few minutes of preparation makes the appointment more useful.

  • 01
    Your current products

    Bring the creams, cleansers and anything bought without a prescription. The names matter more than the bottles.

  • 02
    Prescriptions and reports

    Notes, test results and prescriptions from another doctor save time and avoid repeating what has already been done.

  • 03
    Clean skin

    If the appointment is about the face, come without makeup on the area so it can be seen properly.

  • 04
    Your questions

    Write them down. It is easy to forget the one that mattered most once you are in the room.
