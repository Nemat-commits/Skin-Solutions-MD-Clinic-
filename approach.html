Our approach

A clinic,
not a conveyor belt.

Skin Solutions MD is small by choice. Appointments are unhurried, and the same dermatologist follows your treatment from the first visit onwards.

FCPS-qualified dermatology, medical and cosmetic care, in Blue Area, Islamabad.

Mon–Sat · 11am–7pm · by appointment

01 What we believe

Four things we won't compromise on.

These are not slogans on a wall. They decide how long your appointment is, what we recommend, and when we tell you to stop.

  • 01
    Diagnosis before anything else. Dermatology is often decided by a careful look and a good history. We examine first, and only order tests or procedures where the answer would actually change what we do.
  • 02
    Fewer products, better chosen. A three-step routine used every day will outperform a ten-step routine abandoned in a fortnight. We would rather remove things from your shelf than add to it.
  • 03
    Honest about limits. Melasma, scarring and hair loss are usually managed rather than erased. We tell you the difference at the start, so you can decide with real information.
  • 04
    Continuity, not handovers. Your notes, photographs and plan stay with the clinic. Follow-up reviews are with the same dermatologist who started your treatment.

The consultation

What your appointment actually includes.

  • A full history — medication, products, hormones, family history and previous treatments.
  • An examination, with dermatoscopy where a mole or patch needs a closer look.
  • A working diagnosis in plain language, with time for your questions.
  • A written plan: what to use, how often, what to stop, what it costs, when to return.
  • Photographs, with your consent, so progress is judged rather than guessed.

02 Standards

Hygiene isn't a selling point. It's the baseline.

What you can expect in the room, every visit — no exceptions, no shortcuts for a busy day.

  • Single-use needles and blades; instruments sterilised between patients.
  • A patch test before laser and peels, every time — not only for first-time patients.
  • Written consent for procedures, with downtime explained before you agree.
  • Your records and photographs kept private, and shared only with your permission.

A note from the clinic

It costs us a booking now and then. It is also the reason patients come back with their families.

03 Questions

The things people ask before booking.

If your question isn't here, send it on WhatsApp. A real person reads the messages.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Appointments are strongly preferred, and WhatsApp is the fastest way to get one. We can sometimes fit in a walk-in, but you may wait — and a rushed consultation is not worth the trip.

Is laser hair removal safe for brown skin?

Yes, when the device and settings are right for your skin type. Deeper skin tones need longer wavelengths and lower fluences, and a patch test before a full session. If we think laser is the wrong choice for your skin or your hair, we will say so.

How many laser sessions will I need?

Most people need six to eight sessions, spaced four to six weeks apart, for a good reduction. Hormonal areas — the face, chin, neck — often need more, plus occasional maintenance. Nobody can promise a permanent, total removal.

Will I be pressured into buying products?

No. We recommend a short routine, tell you what each product is doing, and you are free to buy it wherever you like. If your current sunscreen is working, we will tell you to keep using it.

Do you see men and teenagers?

Yes. Acne, hair fall and pigmentation are just as common in men, and teenage acne is one of the most rewarding things to treat early. For patients under 18, we ask that a parent or guardian attends.

I've already seen another dermatologist. Can I come for a second opinion?

Of course. Bring your prescriptions, test results and photographs if you have them. A second opinion is often useful — and we will not repeat investigations that have already been done properly.

How do I pay, and can I get a receipt?

Payment options are confirmed at reception when you book. Itemised receipts are available for insurance or employer claims — just ask at the desk or on WhatsApp.

What if I need to reschedule?

Send a message a few hours before your slot so it can go to someone else. Life happens — we would rather you reschedule than rush through traffic to a consultation you can't concentrate on.

One consultation is usually enough to know where you stand.

Bring your questions. We'll do the examination, explain the options and give you a plan — whether or not you decide to treat here.
