03 Before you come

A few small things that help.

How early should I arrive?

Ten minutes is plenty. If you're running late, send a WhatsApp message — slots are spaced out, and we'd rather adjust than have you miss the appointment.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes. Many patients bring a family member, and it often helps to have a second pair of ears for the treatment plan. For patients under 18, a parent or guardian should attend.

Will I be treated on the first visit?

Sometimes — a simple prescription or a peel can start the same day. Laser and procedures usually need a patch test or preparation first, so the first visit is for assessment. That is deliberate, not a delay.

Do you treat children?

Yes, for common skin conditions such as eczema, warts, fungal infections and acne in teenagers. Bring their previous prescriptions if there are any.

For a severe reaction — swelling of the face or lips, or difficulty breathing — do not wait for an appointment. Go to the nearest emergency department.
